# Perch artwork

`Perch-AppIcon.png` is the bird design approved by the fork owner on 2026-11-01.
It was created for Perch with the built-in ImageGen tool; it does not use the
upstream Vorssaint icon or logo as a reference. `Tools/MakeIcon.swift` renders
the macOS ICNS size family and the in-app BrandMark from this master.

The menu bar uses the actual 🐼 glyph from the Mac's installed Apple Color Emoji
font, rendered at build time into transparent 26 × 20 / 52 × 40 PNGs. The font
is not redistributed. Apple emoji artwork is not claimed as Perch artwork.

## Generation prompt

A sculptural small bird perched on a single refined horizontal ledge, expressed as a confident elegant abstract silhouette with sophisticated folded wing geometry; recognizable as a bird, not a letter or duck. Professional desktop software identity, mature and restrained, not cute, not cartoon, not toy-like, no googly eyes, no chunky outlined shapes. Exceptional industrial-design finish: brushed silver and pearl ceramic bird with subtle dimensional faceted wing, small precise dark eye and understated beak; deep midnight graphite-to-petrol rounded-square macOS icon tile, gentle edge bevel, controlled soft studio highlights, minimal restrained teal reflection, crisp silhouette and carefully balanced negative space. Bird and thin ledge form a compact memorable central emblem, use large clear forms readable at Dock size, no intricate feathers. Straight-on orthographic view, no tilted perspective. Rounded-square tile fills about 84% of the square canvas with symmetrical transparent outside margins, professional macOS optical proportions. Genuine transparent background outside the rounded-square tile. Do not include panda, typography, letters, captions, fake UI, watermarks, extra objects, decorative scenes or original Vorssaint branding.
